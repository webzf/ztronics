Accessories are essential for building, testing and prototyping electronic projects. They simplify circuit assembly, improve reliability and make it easier to experiment without soldering.

This category includes breadboards, jumper wires, pin headers and other useful accessories commonly used with Arduino, ESP32, Raspberry Pi and other embedded development boards.

## Typical Accessories

Some of the most common accessories include:

- Jumper Wires
- Pin Headers
- USB Cables
- Heat Sinks
- Dupont Connectors

## Compatible Boards

These accessories are commonly used with:

- ESP32
- Arduino Uno
- Arduino Nano
- Raspberry Pi Pico
- ESP8266

---

## Why Use Prototyping Accessories?

Good prototyping accessories make electronics development easier by allowing components to be connected quickly and safely. They are ideal for testing circuits, creating prototypes and learning embedded systems without permanent soldering.
