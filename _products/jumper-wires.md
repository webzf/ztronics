**Jumper wires**, also known as **Dupont wires**, are one of the most essential accessories for electronic prototyping. They provide quick and reliable connections between components without soldering, making them ideal for use with a **[Solderless Breadboard](/products/solderless-breadboard/)**.

They are widely used with development boards such as the **[ESP32 DevKit](/products/esp32-devkit/)**, Arduino and Raspberry Pi, allowing sensors, displays and other modules to be connected in seconds.

- Flexible stranded copper wire

- Available in multiple colours

## Types of Jumper Wires

Jumper wires are available in three common configurations:

### Male-to-Male

The most common type, used to connect breadboards and development boards together.

### Male-to-Female

Ideal for connecting pin headers on development boards to modules with female connectors.

### Female-to-Female

Used when connecting two male header pins directly.

Each type serves a different purpose, and many starter kits include all three.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are jumper wires?

Jumper wires are reusable electrical wires fitted with Dupont connectors that allow components to be connected without soldering.

### What is the difference between male and female jumper wires?

Male jumper wires have exposed pins, while female jumper wires have socket connectors that fit over male header pins.

### Can jumper wires carry high current?

They are designed for low-voltage, low-current electronic circuits and are not intended for high-power applications.

### Do I need jumper wires with a breadboard?

Yes. Jumper wires are the standard method for connecting components on a solderless breadboard.
