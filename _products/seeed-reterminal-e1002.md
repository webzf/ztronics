The E1002 is the compact color option in the reTerminal E Series. Its 7.3-inch Spectra 6 panel can display color images and dashboards while retaining the low-power behavior of ePaper.

The trade-off is refresh speed: the E1002 does not support partial refresh and a full update takes approximately 15–20 seconds in Seeed's published guidance. It is best suited to photo frames, notices and dashboards that update occasionally rather than continuously.

## Key specifications

| Specification | Value |
| Processor | ESP32-S3 with 8MB PSRAM |
| Display | 7.3-inch E Ink Spectra 6 full-color ePaper |
| Resolution | 800 × 480 |
| Refresh | Full refresh only; approximately 15–20 seconds |
| Flash | 32MB |
| Wireless | 2.4GHz Wi-Fi and Bluetooth 5.0 |
| Battery | 2000mAh; up to 3 months claimed by Seeed |

Specifications and battery-life figures are based on Seeed Studio's published information. Actual battery runtime depends on refresh frequency, wireless activity, firmware and operating conditions.
