The **Seeed Studio reTerminal E1004** is a 13.3-inch full-color ePaper terminal built around the ESP32-S3. Its large E Ink Spectra 6 panel is designed for information that can remain on screen for long periods: digital photo frames, home dashboards, room schedules, notices and visual status boards.

Unlike an LCD or AMOLED panel, ePaper only needs power when the displayed content changes. That makes the E1004 interesting for always-on information displays, especially when it runs from its built-in battery. The trade-off is refresh speed: Seeed lists a full refresh time of around 20 seconds, so this is not a conventional real-time touchscreen.

## Is the reTerminal E1004 worth considering?

The E1004 is worth considering when screen size and full-color ePaper matter more than interaction speed. Its 13.3-inch panel, 1200 × 1600 resolution and 5000mAh battery make it the largest and most capable color model in Seeed's reTerminal E Series.

The main limitations are just as important as the headline specifications: the E1004 does not have a capacitive touchscreen, does not support partial refresh, and takes around 20 seconds for a full screen update. It is best suited to photo frames, calendars, notices and dashboards that update occasionally rather than continuously.

## 13.3-inch E Ink Spectra 6 color display

The E1004 uses a 13.3-inch E Ink Spectra 6 panel with a resolution of 1200 × 1600 pixels. It can display color images and dashboards while retaining the paper-like appearance and low power consumption associated with ePaper.

The large portrait-oriented panel is particularly useful for artwork, menus, calendars, room information and visual dashboards. It is not designed to replace a fast LCD interface: Seeed's E Series comparison lists a full refresh time of approximately 20 seconds and no partial-refresh support. Its Arduino cookbook gives a broader 25–40-second range for full refreshes on six-color panels. Treat these figures as approximate operating guidance rather than a guaranteed update time.

This refresh behavior should influence the project design. Use the E1004 for information that changes every few minutes or hours, not for rapidly animated widgets, scrolling charts or controls that need immediate visual feedback.

## ESP32-S3 hardware and connectivity

The terminal is based on an ESP32-S3 with 8MB PSRAM and 32MB Flash. It includes 2.4GHz Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 5.0, temperature and humidity sensing, a buzzer and a microSD card slot.

Seeed lists support for microSD cards up to 32GB and includes a 16GB card with the E1004. The board can be powered through USB-C at 5V / 1A, while the built-in 5000mAh battery is intended for low-power operation.

The enclosure measures 376 × 311 × 40mm. This is a complete terminal rather than a bare ePaper panel, so it is better suited to finished wall displays, photo frames and information boards than to compact embedded installations.

## Home Assistant and ESPHome compatibility

The E1004 can be integrated with Home Assistant through ESPHome. Seeed provides an ESPHome cookbook for the reTerminal E Series, including examples for the display and onboard peripherals.

**Important version requirement:** the E1004 display driver requires ESPHome 2026.7.0 or later. Check the current cookbook and generated configuration before flashing, because component support and YAML options can change between ESPHome releases.

The official documentation also provides Arduino examples and support information for SenseCraft HMI. SenseCraft HMI is the no-code option for creating and uploading dashboard content, while Arduino and ESPHome are more appropriate when you want to develop a custom interface or integrate the terminal into an existing system.

## E1004 limitations to understand before buying

The E1004 is a specialist display, and its limitations are important when choosing hardware:

- **No capacitive touchscreen:** the E1004 uses physical touch buttons. If direct touchscreen interaction is essential, consider the E1003 instead.
- **No partial refresh:** the color panel requires a full refresh.
- **Slow screen updates:** a full refresh takes approximately 20 seconds according to Seeed.
- **Not suitable for animation:** rapidly changing dashboards and video-like content are a poor fit for this display technology.
- **Large enclosure:** its 376 × 311mm dimensions need to be considered before planning a wall mount or frame.
- **Battery life varies:** the advertised six-month figure depends on usage and refresh behavior.

## FAQ

### Does the reTerminal E1004 have a touchscreen?

No. The E1004 does not have a capacitive touchscreen. It uses physical touch buttons for basic control. The reTerminal E1003 is the model in the E Series with a capacitive touchscreen.

### How large is the E1004 display?

It has a 13.3-inch E Ink Spectra 6 full-color ePaper display with a resolution of 1200 × 1600 pixels.

### How long does the E1004 take to refresh?

Seeed lists a full refresh time of approximately 20 seconds. The E1004 does not support partial refresh.

### Can the reTerminal E1004 work with Home Assistant?

Yes. It can be integrated with Home Assistant through ESPHome. The E1004 display driver requires ESPHome 2026.7.0 or later.

### Does it support Arduino?

Yes. Seeed provides Arduino examples and documentation for the reTerminal E Series, including the E1004.

### How long does the battery last?

Seeed advertises up to six months from the built-in 5000mAh battery. Actual runtime depends on refresh frequency, wireless activity, firmware and operating conditions.

### Is the reTerminal E1004 suitable for a real-time dashboard?

Not for dashboards that need frequent visual updates. Its full-color panel takes around 20 seconds to refresh and does not support partial refresh. It is better suited to static or slowly changing information.

## Compare the Seeed reTerminal E Series

The E1004 is only one of four devices in the reTerminal E Series. The E1001 and E1003 use monochrome panels with partial refresh, while the E1002 and E1004 use full-color Spectra 6 panels with slower full-screen updates.
