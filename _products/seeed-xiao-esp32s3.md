The **Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32S3** is a very small development board built around Espressif's ESP32-S3R8. Despite its 21 × 17.8 mm footprint, it includes dual-core processing up to 240 MHz, 8 MB PSRAM, 8 MB Flash, 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, Bluetooth LE 5.0 and battery charging. It is the standard version: **there is no onboard camera, digital microphone or microSD slot**.

The XIAO ESP32S3 is easy to confuse with the Sense version. If you need a camera, microphone or microSD, see the [XIAO ESP32S3 Sense](/products/seeed-xiao-esp32s3-sense/).

## What makes the XIAO ESP32S3 useful?

The board is designed for small connected products, wearables, wireless sensors, compact controllers and prototypes where a full-size DevKit is too large. The ESP32-S3 combines two 32-bit Xtensa LX7 cores with Wi-Fi and BLE, while the PSRAM helps with larger buffers, display interfaces and memory-intensive applications.

It supports Arduino, MicroPython and ESP-IDF workflows. The native USB connection is useful for programming and serial communication, and the battery pads plus onboard charging management make portable builds possible.

| Feature | Specification |
|---|---|

| Memory | 8 MB PSRAM + 8 MB Flash |
| Wireless | 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, BLE 5.0, Bluetooth Mesh |
| Interfaces | 1 UART, 1 I²C, 1 SPI |
| GPIO | 11 PWM-capable GPIO, 9 ADC channels |
| USB | USB Type-C |
| Battery | 3.7 V battery input; onboard charging management |
| Dimensions | 21 × 17.8 mm |
| Operating temperature | −20 °C to 65 °C |

The board also includes a user LED, charge LED, Reset and Boot buttons, and a U.FL antenna connector. The external antenna should be connected for reliable radio performance.

## XIAO ESP32S3 pinout: D0–D12

| XIAO pin | ESP32-S3 GPIO | Main functions |
|---|---:|---|
| D0 | GPIO1 | ADC, touch, GPIO |
| D1 | GPIO2 | ADC, touch, GPIO |
| D2 | GPIO3 | ADC, touch, GPIO; JTAG strap function |
| D3 | GPIO4 | ADC, touch, GPIO |
| D4 | GPIO5 | SDA, ADC, touch, GPIO |
| D5 | GPIO6 | SCL, ADC, touch, GPIO |
| D6 | GPIO43 | UART TX |
| D7 | GPIO44 | UART RX |
| D8 | GPIO7 | SPI SCK, ADC, touch, GPIO |
| D9 | GPIO8 | SPI MISO, ADC, touch, GPIO |
| D10 | GPIO9 | SPI MOSI, ADC, touch, GPIO |
| D11 | GPIO42 | Touch, GPIO; no ADC |
| D12 | GPIO41 | Touch, GPIO; no ADC |

**Peripheral defaults:** I²C uses D4 (SDA) and D5 (SCL); UART uses D6 (TX) and D7 (RX); the common SPI mapping is D8 (SCK), D9 (MISO) and D10 (MOSI). Pin assignments can be changed in software where the ESP32-S3 peripheral matrix allows it.

**ADC caution:** Seeed lists nine ADC channels, but its own documentation explicitly warns that GPIO41 and GPIO42 (D12 and D11) do not support ADC on this chip. Use D0–D5 and D8–D10 for analog input rather than relying on D11/D12.

## Power consumption and battery operation

Seeed publishes the following typical figures for the standard XIAO ESP32S3:

| Operating condition | Typical current |
|---|---:|
| Normal circuit operation via USB-C | 5 V / 19 mA |
| Normal circuit operation from battery | 3.8 V / 22 mA |
| Modem-sleep (3.8 V supply) | 27 mA |
| Light-sleep (3.8 V supply) | 2 mA |
| Deep sleep (3.8 V supply) | 14 µA |
| Wi-Fi active | 100 mA |
| BLE active | 85 mA |

These are manufacturer test figures, not a guaranteed current budget for every project. Radio transmission, LEDs, sensors, regulator losses, USB peripherals and firmware activity can increase consumption. Measure your own board in its final configuration before estimating battery life.

The 5V pin is connected to the USB 5 V rail; the 3V3 pin is the regulated output. Seeed states that the 3V3 regulator can supply up to 700 mA, but the practical available current depends on input conditions, thermal limits and the rest of the circuit. Do not feed 5 V into GPIO: all GPIO use 3.3 V logic and are not 5 V tolerant. When powered from the battery, the 5V pin does not provide 5 V.

## Important design notes

- **No camera or audio hardware:** the standard model has no camera, digital microphone or microSD slot. Choose the Sense if those are required.
- **Headers are not fitted by default:** Seeed states that the standard board ships without soldered pin headers. Check the exact seller option before ordering.
- **Antenna:** connect the supplied antenna to the U.FL connector for better Wi-Fi/Bluetooth performance.
- **ADC:** avoid D11/D12 for analog measurements, despite the labels in some pin tables.
- **Power:** the 14 µA deep-sleep figure is a low-power mode measurement, not the current draw while Wi-Fi is connected.

## Programming and software support

The XIAO ESP32S3 can be programmed with **Arduino IDE**, **MicroPython** and **ESP-IDF**. For a first test, install the ESP32 board support package in Arduino IDE, select the XIAO ESP32S3 board profile, connect USB-C and upload a simple GPIO or serial example. For production firmware, ESP-IDF offers finer control over sleep states, radio behaviour and peripheral configuration.

The factory firmware may run a touch-pin LED demonstration. Uploading your own sketch replaces it.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does the XIAO ESP32S3 have a camera?
No. The standard XIAO ESP32S3 does not include a camera. The XIAO ESP32S3 Sense adds a camera, digital microphone and microSD slot on its expansion board.

### Can it run from a LiPo battery?
Yes. The board has battery input pads and onboard battery charging management. Use a compatible single-cell 3.7 V Li-ion/LiPo battery and follow Seeed's connection guidance.

### Is the GPIO 5 V tolerant?
No. GPIO use 3.3 V logic and must not be connected directly to 5 V signals.

### What is the deep-sleep current?
Seeed lists 14 µA typical at a 3.8 V supply for the standard board. Actual system current depends on the battery circuit, peripherals and firmware.

### Are pin headers included?
Not by default. Seeed says the standard board ships without soldered headers; verify the selected seller option.
