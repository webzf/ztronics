Sensors are essential components in embedded systems. They allow Arduino, ESP32 and Raspberry Pi projects to measure physical quantities and interact with the surrounding environment.

In this category you will find temperature, humidity, motion, pressure, distance and environmental sensors. Each product includes detailed specifications, pinout diagrams, compatible boards, applications and related tutorials.

---

## Types of Sensors

Typical sensor categories include:

- Temperature
- Humidity
- Motion
- Pressure
- Distance
- Light
- Gas
- Magnetic

---

## Compatible Boards

Most sensors are compatible with:

- ESP32
- Arduino
- Raspberry Pi Pico

Always check the product page for voltage requirements and supported communication interfaces.
