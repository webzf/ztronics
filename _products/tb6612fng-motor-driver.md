The **TB6612FNG** is a dual H-bridge motor driver designed for efficient control of brushed DC motors. Compared with the L298N, its MOSFET output stage generally produces less voltage loss and heat, making it a strong choice for battery-powered robotics.

## Common Uses

- Two-wheel robots
- Differential-drive vehicles
- Battery-powered robotics
- Joystick motor control
- Small automation projects

## Why Choose It Over the L298N?

The TB6612FNG is typically more efficient and is a better choice when motor efficiency and battery life matter. The L298N remains useful for simple demonstrations and projects where availability and familiarity are priorities.

## Related Project

The TB6612FNG can be used with the [ESP32 Joystick Tutorial](/esp32-Joystick-Tutorial-Read-an-Analog-Joystick-%28KY-023%29-with-Arduino-IDE/) for proportional DC motor control.
