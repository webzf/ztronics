## TRMNL BYOD 7.5-inch DIY Kit: quick verdict

This kit is best understood as an e-paper hardware platform rather than a ready-to-use consumer display. It gives you the screen, controller and battery; you choose whether to use the TRMNL service, integrate it with Home Assistant through ESPHome, or build your own firmware with Arduino.

The main buying considerations are the separate TRMNL BYOD license and the lack of an included enclosure. If you are comfortable with a DIY build, those trade-offs may be reasonable. If you want a finished device straight out of the box, compare it with the official TRMNL device before ordering.

## What's included in the kit?

The manufacturer documentation lists these components:

- 7.5-inch monochrome e-paper display (800 × 480 pixels)
- XIAO ePaper Display Dev Board with XIAO ESP32-S3 Plus onboard
- 2000mAh rechargeable 3.7V Li-ion battery
- 10cm FPC extension cable
- FPC connector

**The enclosure is not included.** The product images may show a case for illustration, but the seller listing states that the kit is supplied without one. Seeed provides open-source enclosure designs that you can 3D print or adapt.

## TRMNL BYOD license: an extra cost to consider

The AliExpress listing states that the TRMNL BYOD license is **not included** and must be purchased separately for **US$50** to access the TRMNL hosted dashboard platform and its plugin ecosystem.

That distinction matters:

- **Using the TRMNL platform:** budget for the separate BYOD license.
- **Using Home Assistant with ESPHome:** the kit can be integrated through ESPHome; this is a separate software route and does not mean the TRMNL license is included.
- **Writing your own Arduino firmware:** Arduino IDE compatibility lets you develop your own application without relying on the TRMNL hosted platform.

Check the current TRMNL BYOD terms and price before purchasing, as service pricing and access conditions can change.

## Home Assistant and ESPHome compatibility

The kit is compatible with **ESPHome**, allowing it to be used as an e-paper display in a Home Assistant setup. Potential uses include displaying weather, energy consumption, room conditions, calendar information or selected smart-home states.

This is not the same as a plug-and-play Home Assistant screen. You will need to configure the firmware, display component and the information you want to show. Follow the official [Seeed Studio TRMNL DIY Kit guide](https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/trmnl_7inch5_diy_kit_main_page/) for the current hardware and setup details.

## Arduino IDE and custom firmware

The XIAO ESP32-S3 Plus controller also supports Arduino development. This makes the kit interesting if you want to create a custom e-paper dashboard, status panel or information display without using the TRMNL service.

If you are new to the controller, see our [Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32S3 guide](/products/seeed-xiao-esp32s3/) for background on the XIAO ESP32-S3 family. Note that this kit uses the **XIAO ESP32-S3 Plus** variant on a dedicated ePaper driver board, so do not assume every pin or board-level detail is identical to the standard XIAO ESP32S3 development board.

## Battery life: what does up to three months mean?

Seeed advertises up to three months of operation from the 2000mAh battery under a deep-sleep use case with a six-hour refresh interval. Treat this as a best-case manufacturer figure, not a guaranteed runtime.

Frequent refreshes, Wi-Fi connections, custom firmware, signal quality and the time spent awake can all change battery consumption. If your dashboard needs near-real-time updates, expect shorter battery life than a display that refreshes only a few times per day.

## Who should buy the TRMNL BYOD kit?

**A good fit if you:**
- Want a large 7.5-inch monochrome e-paper dashboard.
- Prefer a DIY device based on the XIAO ESP32-S3 Plus.
- Want to use TRMNL's hosted dashboard and plugins, and are comfortable paying for the separate BYOD license.
- Use Home Assistant and are happy to configure ESPHome.
- Want to write custom firmware with Arduino IDE.
- Can 3D-print or source an enclosure separately.

**Look elsewhere if you:**
- Expect a finished device with an enclosure included.
- Need a colour display, touch input or fast video-like updates.
- Want TRMNL platform access without paying for the separate BYOD license.
- Need guaranteed multi-month battery life with frequent screen updates.

## TRMNL official device vs BYOD kit

The official TRMNL device is the ready-made route, while BYOD means bringing or assembling compatible hardware yourself. This Seeed kit supplies the main hardware for a 7.5-inch OG-style build, but you still need to account for the enclosure and the separate platform license if you want TRMNL's hosted features.

Choose the official device if you value a finished product and a simpler setup. Choose the DIY kit if you want to assemble the hardware, explore ESPHome or Arduino, or customise the enclosure and software.

## Frequently asked questions

No. The AliExpress listing says the BYOD license is sold separately and lists it at US$50. Check TRMNL's current terms and price before ordering.

### Can I use it with Home Assistant?

Yes. Seeed documents Home Assistant compatibility through ESPHome. You will need to configure the firmware and dashboard content; it is not simply a preconfigured Home Assistant monitor.

### Can I program it with Arduino IDE?

Yes. The kit uses a XIAO ESP32-S3 Plus-based driver board and supports Arduino IDE development.

### Is an enclosure included?

No. The kit is sold without an enclosure. Open-source case designs are available for 3D printing and customisation.

### How long does the battery last?

### Is the display colour or touch-enabled?

It is a 7.5-inch monochrome e-paper display. Do not buy it expecting a colour screen or touchscreen interface.
