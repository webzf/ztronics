## Should You Choose the ESP32-C6-Touch-AMOLED-2.16?

Choose this board if you want a compact **2.16-inch square AMOLED touchscreen** with Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth LE and IEEE 802.15.4 connectivity. It combines a small colour display with sensors, audio hardware, a real-time clock and battery-management circuitry.

The ESP32-C6 is a **single-core RISC-V processor running at up to 160 MHz**. It is a different class of platform from the dual-core ESP32-S3 boards with external PSRAM, so compare memory and processing requirements before choosing it for a demanding graphical interface.

| Board | Choose it when | Main trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| **ESP32-C6-Touch-AMOLED-2.16** | You want Wi-Fi 6, BLE 5 and 802.15.4 in a compact AMOLED board | Single-core C6 and no external PSRAM listed |
| [**ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-2.16**](/products/waveshare-esp32-s3-touch-amoled-2-16/) | You want a dual-core S3 with 8 MB PSRAM | No Wi-Fi 6 / 802.15.4 |
| [**ESP-Mosaico**](/products/esp-mosaico/) | You want the ESP32-S31 platform and modular expansion | Different processor and ecosystem |

## Overview

The Waveshare ESP32-C6-Touch-AMOLED-2.16 combines an ESP32-C6 with a **2.16-inch 480 × 480 AMOLED touchscreen**. It is designed for compact touch interfaces, portable dashboards, IoT controllers and prototyping voice-interaction devices.

The board includes 16 MB NOR Flash, a QMI8658 six-axis IMU, a PCF85063 real-time clock, dual microphones, audio circuitry, a TF card slot and a 3.7V lithium-battery connector.

## Display and Touch

The AMOLED panel has a 480 × 480 resolution, 16.7 million colours, a stated brightness of 600 cd/m² and a 100,000:1 contrast ratio.

The display uses the **CO5300 controller over QSPI**. Capacitive touch is handled by the **CST9220 controller over I2C**.

## ESP32-C6 Platform and Wireless Connectivity

The ESP32-C6 uses a single-core 32-bit RISC-V processor running at up to 160 MHz. It provides 512 KB HP SRAM, 16 KB LP SRAM, 320 KB ROM and 16 MB external NOR Flash.

Wireless connectivity includes:
- 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi 6
- Bluetooth 5 Low Energy
- IEEE 802.15.4, supporting ecosystems such as Zigbee 3.0 and Thread

This combination makes the board interesting for connected interfaces and smart-home prototypes. It does not have the dual-core CPU or external PSRAM found on the ESP32-S3R8 version.

## Onboard Peripherals

The board includes:
- QMI8658 six-axis IMU (accelerometer and gyroscope)
- PCF85063 real-time clock
- AXP2101 power-management IC
- ES8311 low-power audio codec
- ES7210 ADC for audio and echo-cancellation circuitry
- Dual microphones
- Speaker amplifier and external speaker pads
- TF card slot supporting FAT32
- PWR, BOOT and programmable KEY buttons
- USB Type-C for programming and log output

Exposed pads provide I2C, UART and USB connections for external devices and debugging.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does the ESP32-C6-Touch-AMOLED-2.16 include a battery?

There are two official versions. SKU 34201 (-EN) is supplied without a lithium battery, while SKU 34202 includes a 3.7V 1000mAh battery. Check the selected option on the seller's listing.

### Does it support Wi-Fi 6?

Yes. The ESP32-C6 supports 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5 LE and IEEE 802.15.4 for technologies such as Zigbee 3.0 and Thread.

### Does it have PSRAM?

Waveshare lists 512 KB HP SRAM and 16 KB LP SRAM for the ESP32-C6 board, but does not list external PSRAM. If your application needs a larger graphics buffer, compare the ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-2.16 with 8 MB PSRAM.

### Which display and touch controllers does it use?

The AMOLED panel uses the CO5300 display controller over QSPI. Capacitive touch uses the CST9220 controller over I2C.

### Can I program it with Arduino IDE?

Yes. Waveshare provides development resources for Arduino IDE and ESP-IDF.
