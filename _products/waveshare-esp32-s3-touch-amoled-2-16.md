## Overview

The **Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-2.16** combines an ESP32-S3R8 with a **2.16-inch 480×480 AMOLED touchscreen**, 16MB Flash and 8MB PSRAM.

It is designed for compact graphical interfaces, portable HMI projects, LVGL applications and projects that benefit from onboard sensing and audio.

## Display and Touch

The AMOLED panel uses a **QSPI interface** and the CO5300 display controller. Capacitive touch is handled through I2C by the CST9220 controller.

## ESP32-S3 Platform

The board uses the ESP32-S3R8 dual-core LX7 processor at up to 240 MHz, with **16MB Flash and 8MB PSRAM**. It provides 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi and Bluetooth 5 LE, plus native USB through USB Type-C.

## Onboard Peripherals

The board includes:

- QMI8658 6-axis IMU
- PCF85063 RTC
- TF card slot
- AXP2101 power-management IC
- ES8311 audio codec
- ES7210 microphone ADC
- Dual microphones with echo-cancellation circuitry
- 3.7V lithium battery connector with charging and discharging
- PWR and BOOT buttons

## LVGL and HMI Projects

The combination of AMOLED, capacitive touch, PSRAM, IMU and audio makes it suitable for:

- Portable dashboards
- Touchscreen controllers
- IoT interfaces
- Sensor displays
- Compact HMIs
- Battery-powered instruments

## Development

The board supports development with **Arduino IDE and ESP-IDF**. Waveshare provides examples and documentation for the display, touch, sensors, audio and USB functionality.
