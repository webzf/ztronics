## Meshtastic and MeshCore devices

Meshtastic and MeshCore are independent LoRa mesh networking ecosystems. Devices must run compatible firmware to communicate within the same network. Before choosing a product, check which ecosystem is used by your contacts and local repeaters.

The [Seeed Wio Tracker L1 Pro](/products/seeed-wio-tracker-l1-pro/) is available in separate Seeed listings for Meshtastic and MeshCore Companion firmware.

## What to check before buying LoRa hardware

- **Regional frequency plan:** confirm the correct regional band and local radio regulations. Do not assume that a radio's hardware frequency range makes every setting legal.
- **Firmware ecosystem:** check whether the device is supplied for Meshtastic, MeshCore or another protocol.
- **Radio hardware:** compare the transceiver, supported bands, antenna connector and antenna included.
- **Power and battery:** check battery capacity, charging method, solar input and expected operating conditions.
- **GNSS and controls:** for handheld trackers, consider positioning support, display, buttons and phone connectivity.
- **Community support:** check firmware documentation, supported device lists and activity in your local mesh network.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are Meshtastic and MeshCore compatible?

No. They are separate mesh networking systems. A device generally needs compatible firmware to participate in the network you intend to use.

### Which LoRa frequency should I choose?

Choose the regional configuration permitted where you will operate the device. Confirm local regulations and the firmware's regional settings before transmitting.

### Do all LoRa devices include GPS?

No. GNSS is a separate feature. Check the specifications if location sharing or tracking is important.

### Is a handheld LoRa tracker the same as a LoRa module?

No. A handheld tracker is usually a complete device with a battery, enclosure and user interface. A LoRa module is typically a radio component that must be integrated into a larger project.
